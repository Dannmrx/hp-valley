# **App Name**: AltaCare Appointment

## Core Features:

- Fixed Sidebar Navigation: A persistent sidebar to navigate through different sections: Home, Appointments, Available Exams, Doctors, and Location.
- Appointment Form: Form for doctors to fill out patient information: Patient Name, Passport, Phone Number, Consultation Reason, Availability, and Medical Specialty.
- Specialty Selection: A dropdown list of medical specialties for doctors to choose from: Pathology, Orthopedics, Psychology, Psychiatry, Cardiology, etc.
- Location Display: Display location and address details of Alta Centro Médico.
- Doctor Information Section: Section providing info on the doctors that work at the Hospital.
- Exam Information Section: Page about the exam specialties covered.
- Appointment Availability Validator: Use an AI tool to help doctors validate whether their schedule fits into what the patient needs and suggest the best match for the schedule.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A calm, professional blue (#4A8FE7) to inspire trust.
- Background color: Light, desaturated blue (#EBF2FA) for a clean and calm background.
- Accent color: A gentle purple (#9370DB) to highlight key actions.
- Body and headline font: 'Inter', a grotesque-style sans-serif with a modern, machined, objective, neutral look, is appropriate for both headlines and body text.
- Use clear and professional icons for navigation and form elements.
- Maintain a clean and organized layout for easy navigation and form completion.
- Use subtle animations for transitions and form interactions.
